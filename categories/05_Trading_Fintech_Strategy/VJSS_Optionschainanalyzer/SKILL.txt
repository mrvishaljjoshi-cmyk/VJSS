## 1. Tactical Overview
The Options Chain Analyzer (OCA) is designed to ingest raw WebSocket feeds or REST snapshots of option chains and transform them into actionable trading signals. It focuses on Liquidity, Open Interest (OI) Dynamics, and Volatility Surface analysis.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

### SOP-OCA-001: NIFTY/BANKNIFTY Spot Initialization
1.  **Fetch Spot Price**: Always retrieve the current spot price of the underlying index before analyzing the chain.
2.  **Determine ATM Strike**: Round the spot price to the nearest 50 (NIFTY/FINNIFTY) or 100 (BANKNIFTY) to identify the At-The-Money (ATM) strike.
3.  **Establish Analysis Range**: Select 10 strikes Out-Of-The-Money (OTM) and 5 strikes In-The-Money (ITM) for both Calls (CE) and Puts (PE).

### SOP-OCA-002: OI Change Momentum Analysis
1.  **Calculate OI Change Ratio**: (Current OI - Previous OI) / Previous OI.
2.  **Identify Support/Resistance**: 
    - Max PE OI = Primary Support.
    - Max CE OI = Primary Resistance.
3.  **Detect Short Covering**: If Price increases + CE OI decreases = Bullish Short Covering.
4.  **Detect Long Unwinding**: If Price decreases + PE OI decreases = Bearish Long Unwinding.

### SOP-OCA-003: Greek Calculation Logic
1.  **Delta Positioning**:
    - Target Delta 0.45 to 0.55 for Intraday buying (ATM).
    - Target Delta < 0.20 for OTM selling (Credit Spreads).
2.  **Gamma Risk Management**: Monitor Gamma spikes near expiry to adjust Stop Loss (SL) dynamically.
3.  **Theta Decay Projection**: Calculate 1-hour Theta decay to determine if a hold is viable in sideways markets.

## 10. The Greek Mathematical Foundation (SOP-OCA-009)

### 10.1 Black-Scholes Implementation for LLM
To estimate fair value, the OCA utilizes the Black-Scholes-Merton model:
- **C = S * N(d1) - K * e^(-r*T) * N(d2)**
- **P = K * e^(-r*T) * N(-d2) - S * N(-d1)**

Where:
- **d1 = [ln(S/K) + (r + sigma^2 / 2) * T] / (sigma * sqrt(T))**
- **d2 = d1 - sigma * sqrt(T)**

The LLM must prioritize **sigma (Implied Volatility)** as the most dynamic input in the OCA logic.

### 10.2 Delta Sensitivity Matrix
- **Delta > 0.8**: Deep ITM. Behaves like underlying. Used for high-conviction momentum.
- **Delta 0.5**: ATM. Highest Gamma risk. Preferred for intraday scalping.
- **Delta 0.2 - 0.3**: OTM. High Theta decay advantage. Used for credit spreads.
- **Delta < 0.1**: Far OTM. "Lottery" strikes. Usually avoided unless used as a hedge.

### 10.3 Gamma: The Scalper's Friend and Foe
- **Gamma = N'(d1) / (S * sigma * sqrt(T))**
- Highest at ATM. Gamma increases as expiry approaches. This is why ATM premiums move violently in the last hour of trading.

## 22. Automated Script Logic: `options_engine.py` (SOP-OCA-021)

### 22.1 Calculation Logic for Real-time Greeks
```python
import math
from scipy.stats import norm

def calculate_greeks(S, K, T, r, sigma, type='CE'):
    d1 = (math.log(S / K) + (r + 0.5 * sigma ** 2) * T) / (sigma * math.sqrt(T))
    d2 = d1 - sigma * math.sqrt(T)
    if type == 'CE':
        delta = norm.cdf(d1)
        theta = -(S * norm.pdf(d1) * sigma / (2 * math.sqrt(T))) - r * K * math.exp(-r * T) * norm.cdf(d2)
    else:
        delta = norm.cdf(d1) - 1
        theta = -(S * norm.pdf(d1) * sigma / (2 * math.sqrt(T))) + r * K * math.exp(-r * T) * norm.cdf(-d2)
    return delta, theta
```

## 41. Advanced Mathematical Derivations (SOP-OCA-040)

### 41.1 Implied Volatility (IV) Newton-Raphson Method
To find sigma when C is known:
- **f(sigma) = BlackScholes(sigma) - C_market**
- **f'(sigma) = Vega**
- **sigma_next = sigma_current - f(sigma) / f'(sigma)**

The LLM uses this to detect if market makers are overpricing options relative to historical volatility.

### 41.2 Vanna and Volga: The Volatility Greeks
- **Vanna**: Sensitivity of Delta to changes in IV. High Vanna means Delta increases rapidly as IV rises.
- **Volga**: Sensitivity of Vega to changes in IV. High Volga means Vega increases rapidly as IV rises.

## 49. Appendix A: Detailed Option Glossary (SOP-OCA-048)

1.  **ATM (At-The-Money)**: Strike closest to spot.
2.  **ITM (In-The-Money)**: Strike with intrinsic value.
3.  **OTM (Out-Of-The-Money)**: Strike with only extrinsic value.
4.  **IV (Implied Volatility)**: Market's expectation of future volatility.
5.  **OI (Open Interest)**: Number of active contracts.
6.  **PCR (Put-Call Ratio)**: Indicator of sentiment (Puts / Calls).
7.  **LTP (Last Traded Price)**: The current premium.
8.  **Vega**: Sensitivity to IV.
9.  **Theta**: Sensitivity to time.
10. **Gamma**: Sensitivity of Delta.

## 61. The Advanced Greeks Deep-Dive (SOP-OCA-059)

### 61.1 Second-Order Greeks: The Hidden Drivers
While Delta and Gamma are primary, professional OCA operations require second-order awareness:

1.  **Vanna (dDelta/dVol)**:
    - **Formula**: Vega / S * [1 - d1 / (sigma * sqrt(T))]
    - **Usage**: Predicts how delta changes as implied volatility shifts. Critical for maintaining delta-neutrality in high-VIX environments.
2.  **Charm (dDelta/dTime)**:
    - **Formula**: -exp(-qT) * n(d1) * [ (r-q)/(sigma*sqrt(T)) - d2/2T ]
    - **Usage**: Also known as Delta Decay. Helps in understanding how the delta of an OTM option bleeds to zero as expiry approaches.
3.  **Volga (dVega/dVol)**:
    - **Usage**: Sensitivity of Vega to volatility. Helps in pricing "Vol-of-Vol" and managing exposure to volatility spikes.

### 61.2 Third-Order Greeks: The Convexity Layer
1.  **Speed (dGamma/dSpot)**: Measures the rate of change in Gamma relative to the underlying price.
2.  **Zomma (dGamma/dVol)**: Measures the rate of change in Gamma relative to implied volatility.
3.  **Color (dGamma/dTime)**: Measures the rate of change in Gamma relative to time. Extremely high impact in the final 30 minutes of expiry trading.
